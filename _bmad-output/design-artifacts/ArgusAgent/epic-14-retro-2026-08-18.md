## 4. Key Insights

1. **A corrected instrument that finds nothing is a working instrument, not a broken one.** This is the
   epic's central lesson and the hardest to hold. `vacuous_test_ast` went from 31 blocking findings to
   0 on the ratified corpus; that is the removal of **26 false accusations**, and it is also the
   removal of the entire precision denominator. The two facts are the same fact. `DF-13-5-A`
   pre-registered the response **before** the number existed, which is the only reason the response
   cannot now be reverse-engineered from the result.
2. **The most dangerous test in a repository is one that asserts a negative.** `-131` and `-132` both
   passed for reasons unrelated to what they claimed, and both lived in the detector built to find
   exactly that. A negative assertion is satisfied by *every* cause of the absence, not only by the
   one you meant — so a guard asserting a negative must pin the **precondition state** that makes the
   absence meaningful, or it is measuring the wrong thing quietly.
3. **The yield sits in independent re-derivation, not in more care.** Four premises refuted at
   contexting, five defects found at review, **zero** found by the hand that wrote the code. The
   common shape is a stale model, and a stale model is invisible from inside itself.
