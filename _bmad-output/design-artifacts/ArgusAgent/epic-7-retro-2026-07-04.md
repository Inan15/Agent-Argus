## 2. What Went Well (Successes)

1. **The capstone answered the strategic question with a REAL, honest artifact — and the honesty spine held under the highest-stakes review in the project.** 7.2 is where APAA's whole thesis ("honest coverage; prove-not-assert depth; never over-claim") was put on trial against its own externalization claim. The single most valuable outcome: the run says `NOT_READY_FOR_RELEASE` with **0 blocking findings and `grade: demo-heuristic-only`**, and the proof artifact/bundle/Dev Notes state plainly that this is a Tier-A heuristic-only run NOT presented as externalization evidence. A lesser build would have dressed the 2906 findings up as a validated deep audit; APAA refused to, and the adversarial gate independently re-derived every headline figure byte-for-byte and confirmed neither keystone was quietly softened. **The tool practised on itself exactly the discipline it exists to enforce on others.**

2. **The no-source-retention MOAT was proven STRUCTURAL over the REAL Minions tree — and a genuine guard-the-guard correction landed mid-dev.** The first draft of the no-source bundle test used SYMBOL-name sentinels (a class name), which correctly FAILED — because a finding legitimately cites a symbol in a locator's `ast_span` (the FR13 "location"), which is NOT a source-code-body byte. The dev corrected the sentinels to real source-BODY bytes (`import hashlib` / `raise ValueError` / an `HMAC-SHA256` docstring phrase) with a non-vacuity guard asserting each is genuinely present in the audited source first. This is the *correct* 4.3 property (no source BYTES / secret VALUES; symbol locators are in-scope) and it now proves the moat over the REAL repo, not a toy cartridge. The `hardcoded_secret ×2289` findings are advisory LOCATOR records, not retained secret VALUES — the reviewer confirmed `BUNDLE_LEAK: none`.

3. **DF-6-6-A — the marquee 🟠 carried into the runway as the DF-1-7-B analogue — was advanced to its honest boundary across BOTH stories, exactly as the Epic-6 retro sequenced (AI-E6-2).** 7.1 discharged the AUTONOMOUS half (corpus 3→5 genuinely-distinct classes, with each new `rule_id` CONFIRMED-emitted by the real detectors, never a fabricated golden key). 7.2 discharged the EXECUTION half (the real dogfood findings, laid adjudication-ready by the 6.6 match-key shape). The ONLY thing left is the non-code HUMAN adjudication — cleanly isolated as DF-7-2-A. The runway played out precisely as the Epic-5→6 DF-1-7-B closure did: sequence a dated carry-forward to specific owning stories one epic out, and it lands.

4. **Reproducibility-on-a-REAL-repo was proven, not asserted — with a non-vacuous RED-first guard over 2906 findings.** Two dogfood runs over the same tracked content yield byte-identical verdicts + bundle canonical bytes (the builder sorts/order-fixes every collection; no clock/float/set-order in the hashed payload — NFR-D1/P1). The RED-first non-determinism injection (reversed finding order → byte-identity FAILS) is non-vacuous precisely because there are 2906 findings to reorder — the assertion is load-bearing, not a tautology over an empty set.

5. **The dogfood is a committed, byte-stable GENERATOR, not a hand-typed report that rots.** `minions_core/apaa/dogfood/proof_run.py` (`render_proof_markdown`) re-derives `minions-dogfood-proof.md` deterministically from a real audit run. 6. **Reuse-not-fork held at the capstone — the single-serializer AST gate never went red, on the two most-reused-seam stories in the project.** 7.1 imports `partition_repository` (2.4), `build_ast_index`/`compute_loc_by_file` (1.4), `account_spend`/`BudgetConfig`/`baseline_ratio` (3.1) — no reimplemented partitioner/cost-model/serializer. 7.2 REUSES `run_audit_detailed`, `build_evidence_bundle`/`persist_evidence_bundle`, `lint_referential_integrity`, `finding_match_key`, and the 1.1 canonical serializer/envelope BY IMPORT — no reimplementation, no second `json.dumps`, no new hasher, no float. ## 3. Challenges & Growth Areas

1. **The ≥80%-precision externalization gate is STILL PROVISIONAL at project close — and clearing it is the one thing no autonomous story could ever do.** This is the honest headline of the whole project close, not a defect. 7.2 did everything a dev-story CAN do (produce the real findings, lay them adjudication-ready by the golden match-key shape, keep the gate scrupulously un-flipped), but the gate flips ONLY on a recorded human Engineering-Lead + QA-Lead TP/FP adjudication over those real findings per `precision-validation-protocol.md` §4/§5 (DF-7-2-A). Until that human step runs and genuinely reaches ≥80% over ≥5 distinct classes, APAA is DEMO-GRADE, not externalization-ready. The build was scrupulously honest about this at every step — but the gap is real and is the #1 project-handoff item (§11 / §7).

2. **The dogfood surfaced 2906 advisory findings with 0 verdict-eligible — an honest but sobering signal about V1 Tier-A depth.** Because AST-grounding is cut (Tier-A / zero-token), EVERY finding is advisory (`depth_supported=None`), so the real Minions audit produces a lot of SURFACE signal (`cross_partition ×332`, `hardcoded_secret ×2289`, `orphan_code ×285`) and NO blocking depth. This is the correct V1 behaviour and it is honestly flagged — but it also means the dogfood, on its own, cannot yet distinguish a true defect from a heuristic over-emission without the human adjudication. The `hardcoded_secret ×2289` count in particular is almost certainly heuristic-heavy (locator records over auth/HMAC code) and is exactly the kind of finding-class the human TP/FP pass must triage. This reinforces (not contradicts) the DF-7-2-A priority.

3. **DF-6-7-A (live HITL escalation wiring) was deliberately NOT pulled forward — a correct scope call, but it means the Epic-6 retro's AI-E6-3 "wire it in 7.2" recommendation was CONSCIOUSLY declined, not delivered.** The Epic-6 retro named 7.2 as the first live consumer and recommended wiring the 6.7 HITL gate into the live pipeline/CLI. The 7.2 dev made the defensible DN-ESCALATION call that the wiring (config source + `--decision` flag + human-wait transport) is a distinct task beyond "execute the dogfood," and left DF-6-7-A open. This is a legitimate scope decision — but it is the one Epic-6 action item that was RECOMMENDED-then-DECLINED rather than applied, and it should be recorded as such (not silently dropped). It is a clean V1.1/V2 handoff item, not debt.

4. **The `pipeline.py` proactive-split recommendation (AI-E6-4) was mooted rather than executed — fine here, but the underlying size pressure is deferred to whoever wires the live pipeline next.** The Epic-6 retro recommended splitting `pipeline.py` (1090/1200) BEFORE 7.2 wired live call sites. 7.2 did NOT need the split — it CALLS `run_audit_detailed` from a NEW module (`dogfood/proof_run.py`, 750 lines) rather than editing the pipeline, so no split was forced. Correct for this epic. BUT the pressure is real for the NEXT story that actually wires the HITL gate / escalation-config into `pipeline.py` (DF-6-7-A's closure) — that story inherits both the wiring AND the split. Worth carrying the split as a pre-condition on the DF-6-7-A closure story, not re-recommending it in the abstract.

5. **The FIVE-then-SIX-epics-slipping committed pre-`review` test-existence guard (AI-E6-5) reaches project close UN-BUILT — this is the moment it must be RETIRED-WITH-RATIONALE, not re-carried a seventh time.** Every Epic-7 story honored the in-session mandatory-artifacts-before-`review` discipline (zero interruptions, both stories green before flip), exactly as all prior epics did. But the durable committed `*→review` guard was never authored, and there is now NO next APAA epic to build it in. The Epic-6 retro already sharpened this to "build-or-explicitly-retire." At project close the honest call is: **RETIRE it with rationale** — the perfect in-session record across 7 epics + 34 stories (zero test-existence slips ever reached review) demonstrates the discipline held without the tool, and there is no remaining APAA runway to justify building APAA-local tooling for it. ## 4. Key Insights

- **A tool that audits for honesty must be MERCILESSLY honest about itself — and Epic 7 is the cleanest proof that APAA is.** The capstone's most important property is not that it ran, but that it ran and then REFUSED to over-claim: `grade: demo-heuristic-only`, 0 blocking, gate PROVISIONAL, findings handed to a human rather than self-adjudicated. The exact failure mode APAA exists to catch (green CI + a confident-looking artifact masking un-validated depth) is the failure mode its own capstone most scrupulously avoided. This is the whole thesis, demonstrated on the hardest possible subject: itself.

- **"Sequence a dated carry-forward to specific owning stories one epic out" is now a THRICE-proven technique.** DF-1-7-B (Epic-5 retro → closed by 6.2) proved it; DF-6-6-A (Epic-6 retro AI-E6-2 → both code halves discharged by 7.1+7.2) proves it again. The corollary insight, though, is the *limit* of the technique: it works for CODE deliverables. DF-6-6-A's final half is NOT code (a human adjudication), and no amount of sequencing makes a dev-story produce it — the technique correctly delivered everything code-shaped and honestly stopped at the human boundary (DF-7-2-A).

- **The dogfood proves reproducibility is a property of DISCIPLINE, not of the subject.** APAA achieved byte-identical two-run output over a 135-module / 36712-LOC REAL repo with 2906 findings — because every collection is sorted/order-fixed, every figure is int/`Fraction` not float, and no clock/set-order enters the hashed payload. Reproducibility scaled from cartridges to a real repo with zero new machinery, which validates the Epic-1 determinism-spine investment (canonical serializer + content-hashed envelope) as the load-bearing foundation of the whole tool.

- **The character of defer-inflow matters more than the count — and the progress-note convention makes that legible.** Epic 7's raw inflow (3) understates how little NEW debt was created (1), because 2 rows are append-only PROGRESS on an existing defer. Reading the §9.3 metric with the id-prefix convention (`-P*` = progress, not new) alongside a severity breakdown is the right way to read backlog health — a lesson the Epic-6 retro also drew and that Epic 7 confirms.

- **"Prove-not-surface" is the honest line between V1 and externalization — and the dogfood draws it visibly.** V1 Tier-A SURFACES where a defect COULD hide (2906 advisory findings) but does not PROVE any defect (0 blocking, because grounding is cut). The gap between "surface" and "prove" IS the externalization gap, and it maps exactly onto the two open code-boundary defers (DF-6-4-A resolved seam auditor = prove-not-surface for seams; DF-6-3-A resolved call graph = higher orphan recall) plus the human-precision gate (DF-7-2-A). The project's honest self-assessment and its V2 backlog are the same list — which is the healthiest possible place to close.
